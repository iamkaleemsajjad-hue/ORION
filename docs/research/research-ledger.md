## Findings from the supplied plan

- Parameter arithmetic is internally reproducible under the stated no-bias, tied-embedding assumptions: 100,291,840 encoder-body parameters and 306,763,200 decoder parameters. Optional heads are outside the encoder-body target.
- The plan's decoder GQA row has a typographic self-correction about MHA parameter growth; the formula `2 × 960 × (960 - 320) × 28 = 34,406,400` is the correct additional K/V projection count when changing 5 KV heads to 15.
- The byte-level vocabulary split is arithmetically consistent only when all 256 specials and all 256 byte-alphabet symbols are counted inside 32,768: 256 + 256 + 32,256 learned entries.
- Planning throughput, memory, and cloud cost values are assumptions. No hardware benchmark was performed during this software implementation.
- Data-card/license verification in the plan remains a future legal and data-governance task; this software does not fetch or use those datasets.
